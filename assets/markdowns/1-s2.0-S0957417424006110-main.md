Keywords:

Financial distress prediction often accompanies missing sample feature data and imbalanced normal and abnormal samples.To solve misingand imbalanced data that have significant negative impacts on the financial distress prediction model,a two-stage case-based reasoning (CBR)-driven clasification paradigm is proposed to accurately and robustly predict financial distress The proposed clasification paradigm involves two main stages: CBR-driven missng data imputation and learning vector quantization (LVQ)-CBR-driven classifier prediction. In the first stage,the hybrid CBR-driven weighted imputation method is used to fill in missng values in the analytical dataset to obtain reliable and stable imputation performance,thereby solving the data missing problem.In the second stage,theLVQ-CBR-drivenclassification model is constructed to predict financial distress. By highlightingand fully learning minority abnormalsamples,the clasification model solves the low prediction accuracy of minority abnormal samples arising from data imbalance.For ilustration and verification,some experiments are performed on seven Chinese-listed enterprise datasets with diferent missing and imbalance rates.Corresponding results show that the proposed two-stage CBR-driven clasification paradigm can achieve the best imputation performance,greatly improve the prediction accuracy of minority abnormal samples,and integrally realize the best overallprediction performance compared with other imputation methods,imbalanced data processing methods,and their combinations.This implies that the proposed two-stage CBR-driven classification paradigm can be used as a competitive solution to financial distress prediction with mising and imbalanced data.

# 1．Introduction

As the fundamental unit of the national economy and the primary participant in market economic activities,enterprises affect the country's politics,economy,and people's livelihoods (Mai, Tian, Lee,& Ma, 2019).They even play a pivotal role in global asset allocation and economic stability.For example,the collapse of energy giant Enron in 2001 had far-reaching ramifications for the United States and the international community (Munawer, Yahya,& Siti-Nabiha, 2Ol2). The bankruptcy of Lehman Brothers in 2Oo8 triggered the global financial crisis (Dumontaux & Pop,2Ol3).Furthermore,since the outbreak of COVID-19 in 2020,businesses have faced significant challenges to their survival and growth, which have caused serious damage to the economy (Chen & Yeh,2021).During the early stage of bankruptcy,most enterprises exhibit signs of financial distress.Therefore,financial distress prediction is an essential research field in corporate finance,and it is also an important project that financial institutions like banks have been conducting.

Financial distress prediction,also called financial failure discrimination, bankruptcy prediction,and enterprise failure prediction,among others.It plays a significant role in business management decisions, investor investment decisions,creditor credit decisions,bank customer credit ratings,and so on (Sun,Li, Huang,& He,2O14). Usually, financial distress predictions use relevant financial data through mathematical, statistical,or intelligent models to predict whether an enterprise will be in financial distress.However,when predicting the financial situation of enterprises,some feature data may notbe published or provided or can't beunavailable for other reasons,and the number of financially normal samples in the real world is significantly more than that of abnormal samples,so we often face the problem of data missing and imbalance. On the one hand,the missing data affects data quality,taking more time and costs to acquire authentic data. Otherwise,the prediction model can't work normally or obtain reliable classification results.On the other hand,the imbalanced data,especially the small number of financially abnormal samples,leads to the failure to predict abnormal samples accurately. It will bring huge losses to enterprises,investors,and financial institutions.Therefore,it is necessary to construct a classification paradigm that can effectively fill in missing data and improve the prediction accuracy of minority class samples and overall prediction performance.

In the previous studies,Fitzpatrick (1932) began to predict financial distress as early as the 193Os,and Beaver(1966) further studied the univariate model. Subsequently,some scholars introduced multivariate discriminant analysis (Altman，1968)， logistic regression (Ohlson, 1980)，probit regression (Zmijewski，1984)，partial least square discriminant analysis (Serrano-Cinca & Gutiérrez-Nieto,2Ol3） and other multivariate models for financial distress prediction.However, they are all statistical models with strict requirements on data,and input data should satisfy with strict mathematical hypotheses (Mousavi & Lin, 2020). With the development of information science and technology, machine learning (ML） methods were introduced into the financial distress prediction field at the end of the 2Oth century.In the face of complex financial data, ML methods are not limited by hypotheses and have better predictive performance than traditional statistical models. ML methods have been widely used in financial distress prediction. Recently,mainstream models that are constantly improved include the tree model (Kim & Upneja, 2021; Qian,Wang, Yuan, Gao,& Song, 2022), support vector machine (Sun, Fujita, Zheng,& Ai, 2O21; Yao, Hu, & Wang, 2022), case-based reasoning (Sartori, Mazzucchelli,& Gregorio, 2016; Yu & Li, 2023), neural networks (Aydin, Sahin, Deveci, & Pamucar,2022; Wu,Ma,& Olson, 2022),deep learning (Li,Shi, Wang, & Zhou, 2021; Yang, Lim, Qu,Li, & Ni, 2023),ensemble learning (Liang, Tsai,Lu,& Chang，202O；Radovanovic & Haas,2023),and hybrid models (Marso & El Merouani, 202O; Tsai, Sue,Hu,& Chiu, 2021). In addition,some predictive models incorporate text information related to the enterprise to pursue higher predictive accuracy (Borchert, Coussement, De Caigny,& De Weerdt, 2023; Chen, Liao, Chen, Kang,& Lin, 2023).However,in the financial distress prediction field,it is not sufficient for the model to only provide prediction results. It is more important to pay attention to how the prediction results are produced so as to help enterprise managers identify financial risks and take corresponding actions to prevent the actual occurrence of financial distress (Li & Sun, 2Oo9b).In comparison, CBR is an inductive ML method. Its internal mechanism is understood easily,and its prediction accuracy is relatively high (Sun, Li, Huang,& He,2O14). In addition, CBR can guide enterprises to improve management strategies by integrating the operation and development experience of similar enterprises (Li & Sun, 2009c). Thus, considering the performance and interpretability of the model comprehensively,CBR is selected as the foundation and core methodology.Moreover，few existing studies consider the negative impact of data trait problems in datasets on prediction models,especially when two data trait problems coexist.Therefore,based on the idea and principle of CBR methodology,this paper proposes a CBR-driven classification paradigm to solve the problems of data missing and imbalance to achieve excellent prediction performance.In addition, this paper will also explore the negative impacts of data missing and imbalance on each other and together in the experiments.

For the missing data in the dataset,although some new models can tolerate or ignore them (Chen,Huang,Lo, Chen,& Lai, 2022), these models may fail when the dataset has many missing data and small feature data.Various methods can be used to process the missing data. Among them,deleting the row or column of missing data is one of the earliest and easiest methods (Cheng,Kao,& Lin,2O21).However, this method loses a lot of information and is not suitable for small datasets. Usually, some imputation methods apply specific rules to fill in missing data,which are more common and effective.The imputation methods can be divided into single imputation and multiple imputation methods (Yu, Zhou, Chen,& Lai, 2022). On the one hand, single imputation methods generate a unique value to fillin the missing value. Commonly used and mainstream methods include mean imputation (Kurniadi, Rohmana,& Taufani, 2023; Little & Rubin, 2002), $k$ -NN imputation (Cubillos, Wghlk, & Wulff, 2022; Santos, Abreu, Wilk,& Santos, 2020), and regression imputation (Haliduola，Bretz,& Mansmann, 2022; Templeton, Kang,& Tahmasbi, 2021). In addition, some scholars have also introduced clustering (Wan, Razavi-Far, Saif,& Mozafari, 2021), one-hot encoding (Yu, Zhou, Chen,& Lai, 2O22),ensemble learning (Choi, Lim,& Ji, 2023),and deep learning (Lin, Tsai, & Zhong, 2022) into imputation methods.On the other hand,multiple imputation methods replace the missing value with a set of plausible values.Among them,the multiple imputation by chained equations (MICE) method (Hegde et al., 2019; Memon, Wamala,& Kabano, 2023) has been widely used.Recently,other multiple imputation methods have been proposed, such as multiple imputation based on the nearest neighbor (Faisal & Tutz,2021),variational auto-encoder (Ma et al.，2023)，generative adversarial networks (Zhao et al., 2022),and hybrid models (Samad, Abrar,& Diawara, 2022). Obviously,multiple imputation methods are more powerful in performance but have higher time costs than single imputation methods.On the whole,current missing data imputation methods mainly have the problems of poor imputation performance, narrow application range,complicated calculation,and longtime consumption. It is still challenging to simply and effectively deal with datasets with different missing rates,especially for high missing rates. For this purpose,based on the idea and principle of CBR,a hybrid CBRdriven weighted imputation method is proposed to solve the data missing problem and obtain reliable and stable performance.It assumes that enterprises with similar overall financial situations will have similar financial ratios.What's more,two similarity measures are used in the case retrieval process,and weights are assigned to the retrieved similar cases.More importantly,incremental learning realized in the case retention process allows the method efective for datasets with high missing rates.To our knowledge，there is no hybrid CBR-driven weighted imputation method in the relevant literature. This paper proposes this imputation method to fillin the literature gap.

Besides,the quantity of samples in two classes in the dataset is often imbalanced,which leads to the poor prediction of minority class samples for the base classifier.Among them, the quantity of financially abnormal samples is usually small.However, the loss cost of incorrectly predicting the financially abnormal sample is huge.Currently, the methods to solve the imbalanced data classification can be divided into data processing and algorithm processing methods (Yu, Zhou, Tang,& Chen, 2018). Data processing methods are to re-balance the quantity of each class, which can be mainly divided into under-sampling (Farshidvard, Hooshmand，& MirHassani， 2023； Koziarski， 2020)，over-sampling (Soltanzadeh & Hashemzadeh,2021； Sun et al.,2022),and hybrid sampling methods (Garcia, 2022; Zheng et al., 2021).In addition, there are some special resampling methods.For example, Shi, Zhang, Chen, Ji, and Dong (2022） proposed a resampling algorithm based on sample concatenation to transform the imbalanced training dataset into a concatenated dataset in a new sample space.Algorithm processing methods mainly improve models from the algorithm level to enhance prediction performance, such as the improved SVM (Tao et al., 2019), neural networks (Huang, Sang, Sun, & Lv, 2022), ensemble learning (Zelenkov & Volodarskiy, 2O21),and weighted naive Bayes (Kim & Lee, 2023),which are effective.Moreover,some scholars have solved the imbalanced data classification from the two levels of data and algorithm, and their proposed models include the resampling methods and the improved classification model (Chatterjee & Byun, 2023; Gu, Tian, Li, & Jiang, 2022; Kou, Chen,& Hefni, 2022).In summary,no matter what kind of methods, the key is to fully learn the features of minority class samples.Therefore,for the data imbalance problem,an LVQ-CBR-driven classification model is constructed to highlight the minority class samples so that the CBR base classifier learns them better to improve the prediction performance. On the one hand, the learning vector quantization (LVQ) algorithm clusters similar samples using sample features and class labels,which has good clustering performance and interpretability.Each cluster forms a case sub-library in the CBR classifier.On the other hand,the target case will be retrieved and learned in the nearest case sub-library,which can alleviate the data imbalance problem and reduce interference of other class samples,thereby improving classification accuracy and reducing computational time.

Generally speaking,the main motivation and purpose of this paper is to solve the negative impacts of data missing and imbalance on the financial distress prediction model,and based on the idea and principle of the CBR methodology,a two-stage CBR-driven classfication paradigm is proposed to accurately and robustly predict financial distress. It is assumed that the proposed imputation method can obtain reliable and stable imputation performance,the proposed classification model can improve the prediction accuracy of minority class samples,and the proposed classification paradigm can further improve the prediction performance for financial distress as a whole.Correspondingly, there are three main contributions to this paper.Firstly,for the data missng issue, the hybrid CBR-driven weighted imputation method is proposed to fill in the missing data and has the best imputation performance on datasets with low or high missing rates.Secondly,for the data imbalance issue, the LVQ-CBR-driven classification model is constructed to solve the low prediction accuracy of minority class samples and improve the Type I accuracy and overall prediction performance.Thirdly,data missing and imbalance issues coexisting are studied,and the two-stage CBR-driven classification paradigm integrally realizes the best overall prediction performance for financial distress.Moreover, the negative impacts of data missng and imbalance on each other and together are also analyzed in the experiments.

# 2．Methodology formulation

In this section,a two-stage CBR-driven classification paradigm is formulated for financial distress prediction with missing and imbalanced data,as the framework illustrated in Fig.1.In particular,a hybrid CBRdriven weighted imputation method is proposed for the first time to solve the data missing problem and obtain reliable and stable imputation performance in financial distress prediction.In addition,an LVQCBR-driven classification model is also included in the proposed classification paradigm to solve the data imbalance problem，thereby improving the prediction accuracy of minority class samples and overall prediction performance.

As can be seen from Fig.1,two main stages are included in the proposed classification paradigm: CBR-driven missing data imputation and LVQ-CBR-driven classifier prediction.In the two stages,the generic CBR method is first introduced in Subsection 2.1.Concrete descriptions and related methods of the two stages are given in Subsections 2.2 and 2.3,respectively.

# 2.1． Case-based reasoning (CBR)

When confronted with a new problem that needs to be solved, people often look for similar problems to obtain solutions.By recognizing this phenomenon of human behavior in real life, Schank (l983) proposed case-based reasoning(CBR) for the first time.CBR is a set of concepts and techniques that touches on knowledge representation,reasoning, and learning from experience,and it is also a methodology in data mining (Li & Sun, 2O09a; Pal & Shiu, 2OO4). It has the advantages of non-parametric estimation, high stability,and strong interpretability. In the CBR system,past resolved problems are represented as base cases, which forma case library together.Current problems to be solved are represented as target cases.In the case representation of this paper,each case consists of two parts.The first part is the problem description, which is related to the feature values of the enterprise in the dataset.The second part is the solution to the problem,thatis,a certain feature value of the enterprise ora class label indicating the financial situation of the enterprise in the dataset.

In the working procedure of CBR,a 4R cycle is usually used to solve new problems (Aamodt & Plaza,1994). Specifically, the first one is case retrieval. CBR system uses search information to retrieve from the case library and selects base cases similar to the target case.Secondly,case reuse is conducted.The solutions to similar base cases are integrated and reused to solve the target case.Thirdly,case revision is adopted.When the reuse process produces a poor result, the solution to the target case is revised to fit the current problem.Finally,case retention is used.The resolved target case is retained in the case library for future reuse. Among them, case retrieval is the most critical link because retrieving similar cases largely determines the performance of the CBR model.The similarity between cases is currently mainly measured by the feature distance,such as Euclidean distance and Manhattan distance.Considering two cases, $\alpha$ and $\beta _ { : }$ ，the similarity between them can be calculated by the following equations

$$
s i m ( C _ { \alpha } , C _ { \beta } ) = 1 - d i s ( C _ { \alpha } , C _ { \beta } )
$$

or

$$
s i m ( C _ { \alpha } , C _ { \beta } ) = \frac { 1 } { 1 + d i s ( C _ { \alpha } , C _ { \beta } ) }
$$

where $s i m ( C _ { \alpha } , C _ { \beta } )$ expresses the similarity between case $\alpha$ and case $\beta$ $d i s ( C _ { \alpha } , C _ { \beta } )$ represents the feature distance between the two cases.

# 2.2. CBR-driven missing data imputation

The first stage in the classification paradigm is CBR-driven missing data imputation.When missing data exist in the dataset, imputation is a common and effective method to solve this problem.As shown in Fig.1, based on two similarity metrics,the hybrid CBR-driven weighted imputation method is proposed to fill in missing data in the initial sample dataset and obtain a complete sample dataset.

According to the principle and workflow of the CBR method, this paper proposes the hybrid CBR-driven weighted imputation method for the first time.In order to obtain a more reliable and robust imputation performance, two similarity metrics are used to retrieve,which make up for the defects of only using one method.In addition,similar base cases are assigned weights in the case reuse process to reflect their different influence degree on solving problems.What's more,the incremental learning realized in the case retention process makes the CBR-driven imputation method effective for dealing with datasets with high missing rates.The proposed hybrid CBR-driven weighted imputation method is illustrated in Fig. 2.

# 2.3.LVQ-CBR-driven classifier prediction

The second stage of the classification paradigm is LVQ-CBR-driven classifier prediction.Before the prediction,the principal component analysis (PCA) method is first used to process the complete sample dataset.Then, the processed dataset is divided into a training set and a testing set according to a certain proportion. Training samples are represented as base cases to construct the case library,and testing samples are as target cases to evaluate the model performance.Furthermore, the LVQalgorithm is used to cluster training samples in the first step of the classification model.The second step is to use the CBR classifier to predict testing samples based on partial clustering samples.

There are two main purposes for using PCA to process the dataset after imputation.On the one hand,PCA can solve the problems of multicollinearity and redundancy that often exist between financial indicators.This helps to reduce the interference of noisy data and improve classification accuracy. On the other hand,PCA also plays the role of feature dimensionality reduction.It does not only retain the useful information in the original dataset but also reduces the number of features,thereby decreasing the time of case retrieval.It is essential when the number of features and samples is large (Li & Sun,2Oo9b).In addition,PCA,as a linear dimensionality reduction method,also has the advantages of being simple,effective,and easy to implement.

The principle of the PCA method is mainly to map high-dimensional data to a lower-dimensional space through orthogonal transformation (Han,Kamber,& Pei, 2Ol2). In the low-dimensional space, data features with high variance contribution are retained as principal components (Favero & Belfiore, 2O19). The cumulative variance contribution of the retained principal components can be set to $9 0 ~ \%$ to retain as much useful information as possible in the original dataset. Suppose that after the process of PCA, the number of sample features is reduced to $u$ And the new sample feature can be represented as $X = ( p _ { 1 } , p _ { 2 } , . . . , p _ { u } )$ ,where $p$ is the principal component value after dimensionality reduction.

Then, the dataset processed by PCA is divided into a training set and atesting setfor the prediction.However,there is a significant difference in the number of each class sample in the imbalanced dataset, resulting in poor prediction of minority class samples.Therefore,for the data imbalance problem,the LVQ-CBR-driven classification model is proposed to improve the prediction accuracy of minority class samples and further improve overall prediction performance.Fig.3 is the flow chart of the proposed LVQ-CBR-driven classfication model,which mainly includes two steps,and the detailed introductions are presented as follows.

# 2.3.1. Step 1: LVQ clustering

The first step of the classification model is to cluster samples in the training set and collect similar samples together to formulate multiple training subsets, that is,the case sub-library.When solving the target case,it will be retrieved in the nearest case sub-library.These can not onlyalleviate the data imbalance problem and give full play to the role of minority class samples in the training set but also further reduce the time of case retrieval in the second step and improve classification performance.

The learning vector quantization (LVQ) algorithm is selected for clustering in this paper.It is a prototype-based clustering,which characterizes the clustering structure through a set of prototype vectors (Han,Kamber,& Pei, 2Ol2). Unlike general clustering algorithms, LVQ uses class labels of samples to assist clustering,which involves two-fold advantages. On the one hand,it will further improve the clustering performance,which is conducive to the classification prediction of the second step.On the other hand,it can enhance the interpretability of clustering results,which are described in detail below.

(1） Input.In addition to inputting the training set, it needs to preset the learning rate,the number of prototype vectors,and the class label of each prototype vector or cluster.   
(2) Initialization of prototype vectors.According to preset class labels of clusters,samples with the same class label are chosen randomly from the training set as the initial prototype vectors.   
(3) Iterative optimization of prototype vectors.In each iteration,a sample is selected randomly from the training set.Then,the distance between it and each prototype vector is calculated to find the nearest prototype vector.Next,whether the class labels of the two are consistent is judged to update the prototype vector. If the class labels are consistent,Eq.(9) is used to update the prototype vector to reduce the distance between them.Otherwise,Eq. (lO) is used to update.

$$
p \nu ^ { ' } = p \nu ^ { ' } + \eta \times ( X _ { i } - p \nu ^ { ' } )
$$

$$
p \nu ^ { ' } = p \nu ^ { ' } - \eta \times ( X _ { i } - p \nu ^ { ' } )
$$

where $p \nu ^ { * }$ represents the prototype vector nearest to sample $i , p \nu ^ { \prime }$ represents the updated prototype vector after one iteration, $\eta$ represents the learning rate, $\eta \in ( 0 , 1 )$ ：

(4) Stop and output.When the number of iterations reaches the set maximum or the prototype vector is updated very little or no longer, iteration is stopped,outputing the current prototype vector.

After learning a set of prototype vectors,the training set can be divided. Each prototype vector represents a cluster. Each training sample is assigned to the cluster represented by its nearest prototype vector. Then，every cluster is constructed into a case sub-library after completing the division of training samples.

# 2.3.2. Step 2: CBR prediction

The second step of the classification model is to use the CBR model for prediction.In addition to the advantages of non-parametric estimation, strong stability,and being understood easily,the CBR model also has strong interpretability.By introducing the CBR model into financial distress prediction,enterprises can adjust their operation and developmentin terms of similar enterprises to avoid actual financial distress.

As shown in Fig.3, the working procedure of the second step mainly involves case retrieval, reuse,and retention.In the classification model, every case can be represented as $C _ { i } ^ { ' } = \left\{ ( p _ { i 1 } , p _ { i 2 } , . . . , p _ { i u } ) , y _ { i } \right\}$ The first part of the case is the feature values after dimensionality reduction of sample i.The second part is the class label of sample i,which is financial normal or financial distress.The following are detailed descriptions of three main links in the second step of the classification model.

(1) Case retrieval

In this link,the target case is not retrieved in the entire case library but in the case sub-library.For a target case,the distance between it and each prototype vector is first calculated to find the nearest prototype vector and corresponding cluster, that is,the nearest case sub-library. Then,base cases similar to the target case are retrieved from the case sub-library.Euclidean distance is commonly used to measure the similarity between cases.Eq.(1) or(2)is to transform the distance into similarity.Next, seven base cases with higher similarities are selected as retrieved similar cases.For example,Li and Sun (2Oo9b) retrieved 1, 3, 5,7,and 9 similar cases for experiments,respectively.Its results show that retrieving and reusing seven similar cases is the best for short-term financial distress prediction.

(2) Case reuse

For multiple similar cases retrieved, their class labels (the second part of the cases) are reused. Since class labels of multiple similar cases may vary,the majority voting method based on the majority rule is adopted to produce a consistent reuse result as a solution to the target case,which is seen as the prediction result of the testing sample.

(3) Case retention

After a target case is solved, it is retained in the same case sub-library in the case retrieval link. The case revision is not performed temporarily after geting the prediction result (Li, Adeli, Sun,& Han, 2O11). Unless there is other evidence indicating the prediction is incorrect,such as an auditing report from certified public accountants.

# 3. Data and experimental design

To illustrate and verify the effectiveness of the proposed two-stage CBR-driven classification paradigm,relevant data from Chinese Ashare listed enterprises were collected for empirical study.In order to explore the performance of the proposed classification paradigm for financial distress prediction with missing and imbalanced data,several datasets with different missing and imbalance rates are constructed based on the original dataset, comparing some popular missing data imputation methods and commonly used data imbalance processing methods.Accordingly, Subsection 3.1 describes the experimental data, and Subsection 3.2 introduces the experimental design.

# 3.1. Data descriptions

According to the early warning mechanism implemented by the China Securities Regulatory Commission (CSRC),the name of an enterprise with an abnormal financial situation is prefixed with ST or $\mathbf { S } \mathbf { T } ^ { * }$ to indicate that the enterprise has a higher risk.Therefore,this paper defines samples that face financial distress as enterprises that receive special treatment for the first time.In detail,83 enterprises were specially treated for the first time in 2o19 (excluding the financial industry),and their class labels are denoted by $^ { \ast } 1 ^ { \prime \prime }$ .According to the principle of the same industry,same accounting period,and similar asset scale,enterprises with healthy financial situations were selected as normal samples (Wang& Wu, 2Ol7).Moreover, normal samples had no special treatment changes in the past five years.And 166 healthy enterprises were selected,and their class labels are denoted by $" 0 "$ . The sample class ratio of 1:2 is to avoid great potential financial risks in normal samples and reduce noise interference.And healthy enterprises are the majority in the reality.Finally,249 samples were selected to form the original dataset.

According to the financial management theory and relevant studies, this paper selects eleven dimensions indicators to reflect the financial situation of enterprises comprehensively.Firstly,traditional financial ratios are selected,including earning capacity,solvency,cash flow, growth capability,and operating capacity (Jardin, 2O17; Sun,Fujita, Zheng,& Ai, 202l;Wang & Wu, 2017). Secondly,some scholars have also selected indicators in terms of risk level (Sun, Fujita, Zheng,& Ai, 2021), dividend distribution (Tripathy, Wu,& Zheng, 202l),and equity nature (Mai, Tian, Lee,& Ma, 2O19) to reflect the financial situation of enterprises deeply.In addition,this paper also selects economic value added (EVA),relative value,and non-financial index to reflect the enterprises'true worth and performance.For the eleven dimensions,the specific indicators were obtained from the CSMAR database (https://cn. gtadata.com） to construct an enterprise financial distress prediction

index system, as shown in Table 1.

For the original dataset, the ratio of normal to abnormal samples is 2:1,and its imbalance rate is 2.There are 1294 missing data in the original dataset,and its missing rate is $1 0 . 3 9 3 6 \%$ .To further explore the performance of the proposed classification paradigm,three datasets with higher missing and imbalance rates are constructed in the following ways.

Firstly,a dataset without missing data is constructed based on the original dataset. For the missing data in the original dataset,15 indicators $( x _ { 1 1 } ^ { ' } , x _ { 1 5 } ^ { ' } , x _ { 1 6 } ^ { ' } , x _ { 2 2 } ^ { ' } - x _ { 2 4 } ^ { ' } , x _ { 3 0 } ^ { ' } , x _ { 3 3 } ^ { ' } , x _ { 3 7 } ^ { ' } - x _ { 4 3 } ^ { ' } )$ with more missing data are deleted.The remaining 79 missing data are filled with constant zero. Choosing zero is because data are represented as null values in the CSMAR database when some enterprises have no related items or their financial ratios are meaningless.In addition,this avoids the loss of information caused by deletion and the deviation caused by mean and median imputation.

Secondly,the data imbalance problem is simulated by randomly deleting the abnormal samples (Yu, Zhou, Tang,& Chen, 2018). Moreover,according to the idea of missing completely at random mechanism (MCAR) (Little & Rubin, 20o2),the data missing problem is simulated byrandomly deleting the feature values of samples (Yu, Zhou, Chen,& Lai, 2O22). The data traits of the four main datasets are shown in Table 2. This paper sets the missing rate at $5 0 \%$ to simulate the high missing rate situation, mainly referring to Barzi and Woodward (2Oo4). They pointed out that any missing data imputation method is invalid for the datasetwith a missing rate of over $6 0 \%$ .When the missing rate exceeds $5 0 ~ \%$ ，we believeusing less than half of the data to reflect the entire information is not objective.Regarding the imbalance rate,except for the extreme situation,most of the datasets in the literature have imbalance rates between 1 and 3.This paper increases the imbalance rate from 2 to 4 to simulate a slightly higher imbalance rate than the general.The higher imbalance rates are discussed in Subsection 4.4.4. Therefore,there are four main datasets for empirical study by the pairwise combination of missing and imbalance rates.

# 3.2. Experimental design

For comparison purposes,the experimental comparison is mainly divided into three aspects.Firstly,different imputation methods are compared.Five imputation methods are compared with the proposed hybrid CBR-driven weighted imputation method,including mean imputation, $k$ -NN imputation,random forest regression imputation, CBR-driven imputation based on Manhattan distance,and CBR-driven imputation based on Euclidean distance.Secondly,different imbalanced data processing methods are compared.After filling in missing values in the dataset, no-processing,under-sampling,and over-sampling are compared with the proposed method. CBR model is used as the same base classifier.Thirdly,with the increase of missing and imbalance rates, performance changes of imputation methods and imbalanced data processing methods are further compared.

In the experiments,the stratified sampling method is used to divide training and testing sets according to the division ratio of 8:2 (Yu, Zhou, Tang,& Chen, 2O18). In the subset, the ratio of normal to abnormal samples is also equal to the overall imbalance rate.To estimate imputation methods and imbalanced data processing methods,total accuracy,AUC,G-mean, Type I accuracy,and Type II accuracy are selected as evaluation criteria (Barboza, Kimura,& Altman, 2O17; Yu, Yang,& Tang, 2016; Yu, Zhang,& Yin, 2022). Specifically, total accuracy is the ratio of the number of correctly predicted samples to the number of all samples.AUC is the area under the ROC curve that can comprehensively evaluate the performance of methods.G-mean is the geometric mean of Type Iand Type II accuracy,which can avoid the problem of high total accuracy caused by imbalanced data. Type I accuracy is the probability of correctly predicting healthy enterprises,and Type II accuracy is the probability of correctly predicting financial distress enterprises.When comparing imputation methods,the higher the evaluation result, the more beneficial the filling data is to the learning and prediction of the classifier.When comparing imbalanced data processing methods,Type II accuracy can more directly reflect the prediction performance for minority class samples.The higher the Type I accuracy,the more effective the processing method is in imbalanced data classification.In addition, to verify the robustness of the proposed model and eliminate the randomness stemming from random conditions,every model is run 3O times.The final evaluation results used for comparison are average values of 30 results for total accuracy,AUC, Gmean, Type I accuracy,and Type II accuracy.

# 4.5. Summary

# 5．Conclusions

In this paper,a novel two-stage CBR-driven classification paradigm is proposed for the first time to solve financial distress prediction with missing and imbalanced data. In the proposed classfication paradigm, there are two main contributions.On the one hand, this paper innovatively proposes a hybrid CBR-driven weighted imputation method to fill in datasets with different missing rates effectively.On the other hand,an LVQ-CBR-driven classification model is constructed to solve the data imbalance problem and improve prediction accuracy. In addition, the negative impact of the data missing and imbalance on each other and together is analyzed.Accordingly,the proposed classification paradigm consists of two main stages: CBR-driven missing data imputation and LVQ-CBR-driven classifier prediction.

To verify the effectiveness of the proposed classification paradigm, a dataset of listed enterprises in China was collected.On this basis, six datasets with different missing rates and imbalance rates were constructed to further explore the performance of the proposed classification paradigm.Experimental results show thatthe proposed hybrid CBRdriven weighted imputation method achieves the best imputation performance on datasets with low or high missing rates compared with other imputation methods.The proposed LVQ-CBR-driven classification model greatly improves the prediction accuracy of minority class samples on datasets with general imbalance rates compared with other imbalanced data processing methods.For the datasets with different missing and imbalance rates,the two-stage CBR-driven classification paradigm integrally realizes the best overall prediction performance compared with the combination of other imputation methods and imbalanced data processing methods.These results further indicate that the proposed two-stage CBR-driven classification paradigm can be used as a promising tool for financial distress prediction with missing and imbalanced data.

However,the proposed classification paradigm is not perfect,and it still has some shortcomings and deserves further study.Firstly,there is no case revision in either the CBR-driven imputation method or classification model. This may increase imputation bias and introduce noisy samples.Some methods can be explored to revise imputation values and prediction results to further improve the performance of the proposed classification paradigm. Secondly,the proposed LCQ-CBR-driven classification model may not be suitable for extremely imbalanced datasets. Accordingly,other clustering methods can be tried,and classification models specifically for extremely imbalanced can be constructed.In addition,data imbalance and small-sample problems coexist in the dataset,which can be considered.Finally,panel datasets from other countries or regions can be collected for further experiments and studies. We will look into these issues in the future.
